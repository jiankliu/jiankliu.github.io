Visual coding
with spikes.

Reconstructing visual scenes from neuronal activity with a spike–image decoder.

← Back to publicationsRead the paper ↗

The approach

From spikes
to visual scenes.

A neural network decoder maps spikes from a population of approximately 100 retinal ganglion cells to image pixels. The original demonstrations explore recorded retinal responses, simulated spikes and real-time video reconstruction.

Three demonstrations

Archived demo notes.

The original YouTube uploads are currently unavailable. Their descriptions and original links are retained here alongside the available code and dataset.

Demo 01

Reconstructing videos from retinal spikes

Dynamic visual scenes reconstructed from spikes recorded from a population of salamander retinal ganglion cells. The original demonstration compares spike-based reconstruction with reconstruction from fMRI signals.

Demo 02

Encoding and decoding with simulated spikes

Images and videos reconstructed from simulated spikes. An encoder generates spikes using a model of retinal ganglion cells, and a decoder reconstructs the visual scene from those spikes.

Real-time visual coding

Real-time video encoding and decoding using stacked encoders and decoders to reconstruct high-resolution visual scenes.
