01 / PhD questions

Funding & opportunities.

Explore the routes into the lab and funding considerations for UK and international applicants.

What are the current options for doing a PhD in your lab?

We welcome PhD enquiries throughout the year. There are several possible routes into the lab:

  • School-funded studentships. Apply through the relevant school or university funding call.
  • Industry-sponsored studentships. Opportunities may be supported by the lab’s collaborating industrial partners.
  • Self-funded study. One possible arrangement is to cover your tuition fees while discussing lab support for stipend and research costs. Email Jian first to discuss what is available before applying.
Current funding opportunities ↗

Do you have fully funded PhD studentships for UK students?

UK applicants are welcome to enquire about funded studentships. Availability, coverage, eligibility and deadlines depend on the specific opportunity; check the advertised award and discuss options with Jian.

University funding guidance ↗

Do you have fully funded PhD studentships for international students?

International applicants are welcome. Check the particular studentship for nationality or fee-status eligibility and whether international tuition fees are fully covered. Contact Jian to discuss suitable opportunities.

02 / PhD questions

Preparing your application.

What to check, when to get in touch and where to find current university requirements.

I emailed you about possible PhD positions, but did not hear back.

Jian receives a large number of enquiries. Please read this FAQ first, and follow up by email after several weeks if you have not heard back.

Do you require English language tests?

Applicants must meet the University’s current English language requirements for the Computer Science PhD. Use the programme page for accepted tests, current scores and any applicable alternatives.

Current English language requirements ↗

Do I need a master’s degree to apply for the PhD?

A master’s degree is not a blanket prerequisite in the current Computer Science PhD entry requirements. Please check the programme page for the degree requirements and equivalent international qualifications, and contact admissions if your circumstances need clarification.

Academic entry requirements ↗

When should I prepare the application?

Get in touch when you are ready to discuss your research interests. Own-proposal applications are accepted throughout the year, while advertised projects and funding calls have their own deadlines. Allow time for the application and supporting documents.

How to apply ↗Advertised research projects ↗

How many years do I need to get my PhD?

The University currently describes the Computer Science PhD as typically three years full-time. Studentship funding periods and any writing-up arrangements vary; check your programme and funding offer. Discuss part-time study and its duration with admissions.

03 / PhD questions

Research & life in the lab.

Bring your questions, explore your interests and hear from the people doing the work.

What are you studying? Can I bring my ideas for a possible PhD project?

Yes. You are welcome to bring your own ideas and projects. The lab works across neural computation and machine learning with collaborators worldwide, and develops an individual research plan with each member. Explore the research and highlights pages to find shared interests.

Research areas →Research highlights →

How many papers should I publish to get my PhD?

The lab does not set a fixed publication count. We support publishing work when it is ready, and publications can help communicate your research and support future academic or industry applications. Degree requirements are determined by the University.

How can I learn more about the lab and the PI’s supervision style?

Please contact current lab members. They can share their experience of the research environment, working with the PI and life in the lab.
