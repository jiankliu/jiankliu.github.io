01 / Our research

From mechanisms
to possibilities.

We combine neural recordings, computational modelling and machine learning to explore how brains compute, learn and adapt.

01

Understanding neural systems

Computational
brain science

How do neural systems support perception, learning, memory, decisions and movement? We investigate recordings from animals and humans, and models of dendrites, neurons, circuits and brain areas.

Neural coding · Learning · Neural dynamics
02

Connecting models to medicine

Computational
medicine

Working with clinicians, we apply machine learning to molecular data and human brain recordings to investigate brain disease and support research into medicines and therapies.

Brain health · Proteins · Machine learning
