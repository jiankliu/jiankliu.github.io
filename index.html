What we do

Research

We combine computational modelling, data analysis and machine learning to study how neural systems work and to develop approaches for health and medicine.

Computational brain science

We investigate perception, learning and memory, decision-making and motor control. Our work spans brain recordings from animals and humans and models of dendrites, neurons, circuits and brain areas.

Computational medicine

In collaboration with clinicians, we apply machine learning to molecular data and human brain recordings to investigate brain disease and support the design of medicines and therapies.
